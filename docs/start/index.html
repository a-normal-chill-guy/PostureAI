Get Started
with PostureAI

From download to better posture in about five minutes.

Download the latest release Read the FAQ
1

Check your Mac

  • Camera mode: any Mac running macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later, with a built-in or external webcam.
  • AirPods mode: macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later with AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, or AirPods 3rd generation or newer.

No account, sign-in, or subscription is required.

2

Download and install

Grab PostureAI-vX.Y.Z.dmg (or the .zip) from the latest release, then drag PostureAI.app into your Applications folder.

First launch only: because the direct-download build is not notarized with Apple, macOS will ask you to confirm. Right-click PostureAI.app in Applications and choose Open, then confirm. If the app is still blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. This is a one-time step — every future update installs without warnings.
3

Grant permissions

On first run, PostureAI asks for exactly what its features need:

  • Camera — for camera-based posture monitoring
  • Motion & Fitness — for AirPods head-tilt tracking
  • Bluetooth — to detect your AirPods

You can decline any of these and grant them later in System Settings → Privacy & Security; only the related feature turns off. Everything is processed on your Mac — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

4

Calibrate

Sit up straight in your natural working position and follow the onboarding calibration. PostureAI learns your baseline so the detection matches your body, not a generic template.

Sitting position changed? Click the menu bar icon → Recalibrate anytime.

5

Work as usual

That’s it. When you slouch past your threshold, the screen gradually blurs; sit up straight and it clears instantly. Fine-tune sensitivity, reminder style, sounds, and breaks from Settings in the menu bar menu.

Updates arrive automatically — when an update is available you’ll be asked once, and PostureAI restarts itself afterwards.

AirPods posture tracking requires AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, or AirPods 3rd generation or newer, with head-tracking enabled. Apple, AirPods, Vision, and CoreMotion are trademarks of Apple Inc., referenced here only to describe compatible platforms and technologies.
