Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about installing, permissions, tracking, and updates.

Why does macOS say PostureAI “can’t be opened”?

Direct-download builds are not notarized with Apple. This is expected and safe for a first launch:

  • Right-click PostureAI.app in Applications and choose Open, then confirm.
  • If still blocked: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.

This is required once per install — automatic updates afterwards install without any warnings.

Which headphones work with AirPods mode?

AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, and AirPods 3rd generation or newer — they contain the head-tracking motion sensors PostureAI reads. You’ll also need macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later for this mode.

Other headphones aren’t supported yet; broader motion-headphone support is on the roadmap.

Does camera mode work on macOS 13?

Yes — camera-based posture monitoring works on macOS 13.0 (Ventura) and later. Only AirPods head-tracking requires macOS 14+.

I denied a permission by mistake. How do I fix it?

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera (or Motion & Fitness, Bluetooth) and toggle PostureAI back on. The related feature resumes immediately — no reinstall needed.

The blur never appears. What should I check?

  • Recalibrate from the menu bar while sitting up straight — an old baseline is the most common cause.
  • Raise sensitivity or lower the dead zone in Settings.
  • Check that monitoring is ON and you’re not paused (battery saver, Focus mode, or presenting).
  • Camera mode: make sure your face/head is visible in frame with decent lighting.

Is my camera recording me?

No. Frames are analyzed in memory, moment to moment, to estimate pose — nothing is ever saved, recorded, or transmitted. You can verify: the green camera indicator appears only while monitoring is active, and there is no video file anywhere on disk.

How much battery does it use?

Very little. Detection runs in short bursts rather than continuously, AI tasks pause automatically when you’re on battery power, and the app idles completely while monitoring is off.

Can I pause tracking temporarily?

Yes — toggle monitoring from the menu bar, use your global hotkey if configured, enable pause during Focus, or turn on presenting mode so blur never interrupts screen sharing.

How do I reset or delete everything?

Quit PostureAI, drag it out of Applications, and delete ~/Library/Application Support/PostureAI. That removes statistics, usage logs, calibration, and any downloaded AI model — there is nothing stored on any server.

How do updates work?

The built-in updater checks daily for new releases on GitHub, shows you what changed, and installs after you approve — every download is cryptographically signed and verified. You can also check manually anytime from the menu bar. Mac App Store builds update through the App Store instead.
